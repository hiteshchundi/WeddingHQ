## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies the quiet competence and warmth of a master coordinator's bespoke clothbound notebook transformed into modern digital software. Designed specifically for the layered complexities of Indian weddings—spanning multiple days, distinct families, ceremonies, and logistical dependencies—the interface rejects chaotic bridal commercialism in favor of grounded reassurance, tactile dignity, and calm focus. 

The emotional signature is **60% warm and human, 40% premium and understated**. It respects that planning a wedding is high-stakes emotional work and serious logistical management. The UI feels like natural cotton-rag paper, warm ink, and deliberate editorial craftsmanship.

### Aesthetic Movement & Expression
The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Editorial Minimalist** with **Warm Craft Neoclassicism**:
- **Paper, Not Glass:** Frosted glass, blurs, heavy dropshadows, and neon gradients are discarded. Visual separation relies on subtle surface warmth, fine stone hairlines, and generous whitespace.
- **Tone-Downed Cultural Grounding:** Traditional celebration motifs are honored through earthy, botanical linework and architectural balance rather than loud festive kitsch.
- **Strict Anti-Patterns:**
  - No hot pink, intense crimson, or oversaturated festive marigold.
  - No floral clipart, paisley borders, or generic mandala frame clutter.
  - No faux-luxury calligraphy, brush scripts, or ornamental cursive fonts.
  - No corporate SaaS royal blue, glowing buttons, or floating card overload.

## Colors

### Color Hierarchy & System Roles
- **Primary (`#5E7057`)**: Muted Sage / Olive Green. Anchors key interactive moments, primary confirmations, navigation highlights, and operational status states. Grounded, organic, and restorative.
- **Secondary (`#B4654A`)**: Clay / Terracotta. Reserved strictly for delicate emotional highlights, micro-badges, urgent logistical callouts, and overdue markers. Used with extreme restraint (never for full-width structural fills).
- **Tertiary (`#D8D2C5`)**: Warm Stone. Used for subtle structural container accents, divider rules, subtle interactive hovers, and table borders.
- **Neutral (`#2C2B29`)**: Soft Charcoal. Replaces pure black entirely to eliminate stark electronic contrast on warm ivory canvases.

### Palette Modalities

#### 1. Light Mode (Default Canvas)
- **Base Canvas (`canvas-light`)**: `#FBF9F5` provides a warm, natural ivory paper tone.
- **Surfaces & Cards (`surface-light`)**: `#F5F2EB` offers subtle contrast without requiring artificial shadow offsets.
- **Hairline Borders (`border-light`)**: `#E6E2D8` defines structure with 1px tactile stone strokes.

#### 2. Dark Mode
- **Base Canvas (`canvas-dark`)**: `#1E1D1B` deep warm charcoal. Eliminates harsh cold blacks.
- **Surfaces (`surface-dark`)**: `#262422` layered stone containers.
- **Interactive Accent (`primary-dark`)**: `#7D9274` an elevated, desaturated sage tuned for WCAG AAA legibility against dark slate.

#### 3. Eye Comfort Mode
An intentional low-glare UI mode designed for late-night coordination sessions. It warms the UI palette, reduces cool-toned accents, and softens hairline dividers to `#DDD6C7` without shifting colors to an unreadable amber.

## Typography

### Typographic Intent
The system employs a strict separation between **Operational UI** and **Emotional Editorial Moments**:
- **Body & Interface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, contemporary sans-serif with humanist curves. Drives all forms, tables, counters, schedules, and management hierarchies. Provides utmost clarity for logistical workloads and numeric notations (e.g. ₹ Lakh/Crore expressions).
- **Ceremonial & Hero Accent (`Newsreader`)**: Reserved exclusively for couple naming conventions (`Ravi & Priya`), guest invitation greetings, and hero chapter title moments. It provides timeless, dignified literary warmth without descending into generic wedding-script calligraphy.

### Structural Rules
- All numerical stats and currency figures must render in `Plus Jakarta Sans` to guarantee tabular alignment and instant legibility.
- Never use `Newsreader` for button labels, modal headers, navigation tabs, or form labels.
- Micro-labels (`label-sm`) utilize slight letter spacing (`0.04em`) and uppercase styling for date-pills and status indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy
The spatial system uses an **8px base rhythm** with a **4px micro-grid** for compact form assemblies. Layouts prioritize quiet, uncluttered reading surfaces over high-density dashboard layouts.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Models
- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 
  - Dual-zone interface: A persistent 260px collapsible warm-stone navigation rail paired with a max-width `1200px` content canvas.
  - Generous outer section margins (`margin`: `2.5rem`) ensure the platform breathes like an open journal.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**:
  - Sidebar collapses to an icon rail with warm tooltips.
  - Multi-column tables collapse to prioritized household and guest summaries.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**:
  - Single-column canvas with a sticky, thumb-friendly 5-item bottom sheet bar.
  - Margin contracts to `1rem` to maximize usable screen width for event timelines and RSVP check-offs.

## Elevation & Depth

### Depth Philosophy
Depth is created through **tonal surfaces and tactile physical layering**, never deep drop shadows or glassmorphism. The system replicates high-grade stationary: sheets layered upon binders.

### Layering Rules
- **Base Canvas:** Lowest plane (`#FBF9F5`). Acts as the desk or notebook cover.
- **Content Cards:** Resting surface (`#F5F2EB`), bordered by a tactile `1px solid #E6E2D8` border. No drop shadow is applied at rest.
- **Lifted Elements (Flyouts, Date Pickers, Menus):** Use a single subtle, wide-diffusion warm shadow to indicate detachment:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(44, 43, 41, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(44, 43, 41, 0.03);`
- **Modal Dialogs & Bottom Drawers:** High z-index surfaces layered over a warm charcoal semi-translucent scrim (`rgba(30, 29, 27, 0.4)`), using an outline border rather than dramatic glow effects.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Radii
The system uses restrained, architectural corners (`roundedness: 1`). Radii stay between **4px and 8px** to mirror trimmed cardstock.

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Select Menus:** Strict `6px` radius (`0.375rem`).
- **Cards, Panels, and Sheets:** `8px` radius (`0.5rem`).
- **Micro-Badges & Status Tags:** `4px` subtle radius. Full-height pill radius (`9999px`) is reserved exclusively for numerical counts (e.g., guest counters) and circular avatar representations.
- Excessive bubble radii and rounded bubbly cards are prohibited.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid muted sage (`#5E7057`) with warm off-white text (`#FBF9F5`), 1px subtle matching border, `height: 40px`, padding `0 18px`, `border-radius: 6px`. Hover state subtly deepens tone to `#4D5D47`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with warm stone border (`1px solid #D8D2C5`), text in `#2C2B29`. Hover adds a delicate wash of `#EFEBE2`.
- **Destructive:** Soft terracotta text (`#B4654A`) with an optional soft tint background on hover (`rgba(180, 101, 74, 0.08)`). No bright fire-engine red.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Single-line and textarea controls use `surface-light` (`#F5F2EB`) fill with `1px solid #D8D2C5`. Height `42px`, internal padding `10px 14px`.
- **Focus State:** 1px stroke of primary sage (`#5E7057`) with an ambient outer ring of `2px rgba(94, 112, 87, 0.15)`. No default browser blue rings.
- Floating helper text sits cleanly below inputs in `text-secondary-light` (`#706D65`).

### Cards & Event Panels
- Flat containers with `#F5F2EB` background, enclosed by `1px solid #E6E2D8`.
- Card padding set to `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- Section headers within cards use `headline-sm` with optional subtle bottom hairline separators rather than heavy colored header blocks.

### Chips & Status Tags
- **Event Status (Confirmed/Tentative):** Compact `height: 24px`, font size `11px`, `font-weight: 600`.
- **Coloring:** Soft sage tint background (`#EEF2EC`) with sage text (`#4D5D47`) for completed tasks; soft terracotta tint (`#F7ECE8`) with terracotta text (`#B4654A`) for pending or action-required states.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square checkbox (`18px × 18px`) with `4px` corner radius. Radio circle (`18px × 18px`).
- Unselected: Crisp 1.5px border of `#D8D2C5` on clean canvas.
- Selected: Solid fill of `#5E7057` with custom crisp white check icon (`stroke-width: 2px`).

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Clean row architecture separated by `1px solid #E6E2D8` hairlines.
- Generous vertical cell padding (`14px 0`) ensuring effortless tapability on mobile touch screens.
- Avoid multi-colored zebra row striping.

### Specialized Wedding System Components
- **Household Grouping Card:** Organizes multi-generational guests under family heads with clear count summaries (e.g., `Sharma Family • 4 Attending`) using `label-md` tags.
- **Ceremony Timeline Rail:** A vertical 1px stone thread connecting ceremonial nodes (Mehendi, Haldi, Sangeet, Muhurtham) with muted sage indicators, replacing carnival-like iconography with calm chronological structure.
